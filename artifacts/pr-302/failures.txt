- Owner allow rules, per-agent overrides, saved grants or resumed sessions bypass Ask/Auto.
- Denied command/edit executes, or Auto receives no approval request.
- Alphabetical model list overrides engine default; learned ACP default is discarded.
- Unsupported efforts are offered for a model.
- Failed CLI output becomes a model; failed/partial logout reports success.
- Logout omits provider/credential IDs, removes environment credentials, or leaves ACP alive.
- No-account models become unavailable after logout.
- Configured CLI command/arguments are ignored.
