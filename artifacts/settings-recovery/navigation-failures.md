# Settings navigation inventory

Before the production-browser journey:

- A section fails in a fresh installation with no computer, account, usage, people or history.
- Desktop section links or the phone section picker open the wrong route or keep the wrong selected label.
- A narrow viewport clips the page or makes its primary controls unreachable.
- Theme changes, client navigation, or repeated section switches throw an uncaught exception.
- An empty state offers no explanation, or loading never settles.
- Leaving a section during asynchronous work updates a disposed view or retains its subscriptions.

Use a disposable home, no runner or real account, deterministic embeddings, and a production build. Record each route, selected section, document width and browser errors in result.json. Browser viewport checks do not establish physical keyboard or screen-reader acceptance.
